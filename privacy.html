Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

MakerKit3D is committed to protecting your online privacy. This privacy document outlines the types of data collected and how browser-side processing handles your files.

Client-Side Data Processing

All 3D mesh computations, image processing for lithophanes, and STL file generations are executed 100% locally within your browser using WebGL and WebAssembly. No text, uploaded personal photos, or CAD parameters are transmitted or saved to external servers.

Cookies and Advertising

We may use third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on prior visits to our site or other internet locations.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact Page.
