Slicer Optimization for Parametric Models

Ensure maximum dimensional accuracy and layer bonding when slicing models generated on STLKit3D.

1. Wall Line Count & Shell Integrity

For functional parts like our Parametric Storage Box and Desk Cable Clip, strength comes primarily from perimeters rather than infill density:

  • Set Wall Line Count / Perimeters to a minimum of 3 (or 4 for snap-fit clips).
  • Set Top and Bottom solid layers to at least 4 to ensure watertight tops.

2. First Layer Squish & Dimensional Tolerances

Parts with tight tolerances or press-fit clips require zero elephant's foot distortion:

  • Enable Initial Layer Horizontal Expansion (or Elephant Foot Compensation) set to -0.15 mm.
  • Keep initial layer line width around 100% to 110% to maintain dimensional precision at the base.

3. Infill Patterns for Functional Strength

For mechanical models generated with our tools, choose isotropic infill patterns:

  • Gyroid: Offers uniform strength in all three axes without high nozzle resistance.
  • Cubic: Great strength-to-weight ratio for rigid storage containers.

4. Lithophane Slicing Rules

If printing light-transmitting lithophanes generated by our tool:

  • Always print with 100% Infill (or 10 walls) to avoid internal grid shadows.
  • Print vertically on the Y-axis for bed slingers to minimize surface ringing.
