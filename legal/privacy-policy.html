Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At STLKit3D (accessible from https://stlkit3d.com), your privacy is one of our primary commitments. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is processed when using our client-side parametric 3D utilities.

1. Client-Side Processing Architecture

All model generators—including keychain text rendering, parametric box dimensions, and lithophane image processing—execute entirely within your web browser using HTML5, WebGL, and JavaScript. Your input text and uploaded photographic files are never uploaded to, stored on, or inspected by our servers.

2. Cookies and Advertising Partners

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to STLKit3D and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings (https://www.google.com/settings/ads).

3. Log Files

Like standard web platforms, STLKit3D's hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages) may automatically log non-personally identifiable requests, including IP addresses, browser types, internet service providers (ISPs), date/time stamps, and referring pages for maintenance and security monitoring.

4. Contact Information

If you have questions about this policy, contact us directly at Sobl5910@gmail.com.
